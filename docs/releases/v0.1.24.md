# Release v0.1.24

**A process release: a multi-agent review standard, its adoption record, an
optional review-journal contract, and one Engineering Principle Record on the
integrity of durable claims.**

## Overview

v0.1.24 adds a review methodology and two decision records, plus a build guard
for the hand-maintained release surfaces. No capability-token bump.

## Highlights

### Fierce-Collaboration Review standard (0.4.0, draft)

A review methodology for AI-agent and human panels that is adversarial in
verification and collaborative in goal, so a relied-upon review's rigor does not
depend on who ran it. It covers seat composition, disposition vocabulary,
provenance anchors, and an optional machine-readable journal with
sensitivity-tagged records.

Anchor token forms are explicit about what a digest is a digest _of_. A
composite anchor names the object format that produced it —
`git-tree:<object-format>:<oid>` — read from the repository rather than
assumed, so the label names the algorithm that actually produced the value.

### PDR-0005: Adopt the fierce-collaboration multi-agent review process

Status: proposed.

The record carries a table of **accepted contract gaps**, each with an owner and
a closure trigger, disclosed on the contract surface itself so a downstream
reader working from a pinned reference can see them without access to any
panel's working record. Until a trigger fires, the corresponding claim is stated
as design intent and the gap travels with the contract. A conforming journal is
evidence of shape, not of these properties.

### `review-journal/v0` contract

Manifest and event schemas, examples, and a contract manifest. Examples are
validated in `lint-config`.

Stability: **experimental** under
[schema-stability-classification](../standards/schema-stability-classification.md)
— v0.x, breaking changes possible without notice, for evaluation only.

### EPR-0003: Durable claim integrity

Status: proposed.

> A durable claim MUST state no more than its mechanism and its evidence
> support, and MUST co-move with them.

This lane's third Engineering Principle Record, and a sibling to EPR-0002 rather
than a child of it: EPR-0002 governs whether a gate's verdict can be trusted,
EPR-0003 governs whether a claim's wording is bounded by what produced it.

### Release-surface guard

`make check` asserts that the hand-maintained release surfaces moved with
`VERSION`: `RELEASE_NOTES.md` leads with the current version,
`docs/releases/vX.Y.Z.md` exists, and `CHANGELOG.md` carries the version's
section, defines its compare link, and points `[unreleased]` at it. Ships with
negative controls that pin each rejection to its own assertion.

## Upgrade Notes

Documentation and schemas only. No action required for existing consumers.

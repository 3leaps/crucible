# Release v0.2.7

**Adds proposed `identity-evidence/v0` and draft `seat-spheres/v0` contracts, extends `project-work/v0` with commitment and `agent`/`run` provenance fields, adds the draft `advisor` role, splits SPDX `MIT`/`CC0-1.0` license files, and enforces pre-sign tagger identity.**

This release introduces the proposed `identity-evidence/v0` contract and four
categorical identity-assurance classifier dimensions, adds the draft
`seat-spheres/v0` standing-delegation contract and companion model standard, and
extends `project-work/v0` with `requested_by`, `check_by`, and `agent` and `run`
party kinds. It also adds the supplemental draft `advisor` role prompt, splits
root licensing into verbatim SPDX `LICENSE` (`MIT`), `LICENSE-CC0` (`CC0-1.0`)
and `NOTICE`, requires pre-sign tagger identity and signing-subkey verification
in `scripts/release-tag.sh`, and tightens `durable-record` (`v0` and `v1`)
wire-preflight ordering for large non-protocol integers.

## Highlights

### Proposed `identity-evidence/v0` contract and identity-assurance classifiers

- **Contract and classifiers.** Adds
  [`docs/standards/identity-evidence-contract.md`](../standards/identity-evidence-contract.md),
  [`docs/standards/identity-assurance-classification.md`](../standards/identity-assurance-classification.md),
  four categorical classifier definitions under `config/classifiers/dimensions/`
  (`identity-binding`, `key-custody`, `execution-isolation`,
  `platform-evidence`), and the `schemas/identity-evidence/v0/` schema, example
  and vector bundle.
- **Observation and report model.** Defines immutable observation and evaluated
  report snapshots with separate enrollment identifiers and key descriptors,
  directed execution-isolation boundaries, exact platform-property claim
  references, digest-pinned `input_reports` provenance, and per-event optional
  `time_attribution`.
- **Profiles and controls.** Includes a local process/session launch-binding
  profile (`profiles/local-process-session/`) and an experimental 256 KiB /
  depth-16 wire admission profile (`profiles/report-admission/`) with shared
  wire vectors and batched logical/wire control scripts
  (`scripts/test-identity-evidence-controls.py` and
  `scripts/test-identity-wire-controls.py`).

### Draft `seat-spheres/v0` contract and model

- **Seat view and standing delegation.** Adds
  [`docs/standards/seat-spheres-model.md`](../standards/seat-spheres-model.md) and
  [`schemas/seat-spheres/v0/`](../../schemas/seat-spheres/v0/README.md)
  (`contract.json`, `seat-view.schema.json`, 4 positive examples, 40 negative
  reject fixtures and `scripts/test-seat-spheres-controls.sh`). A seat view pairs
  a closed seat `function` (`builder`, `operator`, `steward`, `consultant`,
  `analyst`) with optional `reports_to`, optional `almanac_locator`, and
  per-subject `spheres[]` (`subject {kind, id}`, closed `relation` `lead` |
  `support` | `advise` | `assure` | `interested`, optional allow/deny
  `exceptions[]` with required `reason` on `allow`, and optional `since`, `note`,
  `cover`, `review_by`, `no_apply` and expiring `measures[]`).
- **Rollup and `project-work/v0` seam.** Specifies read-time rollup rules,
  closed-by-default subject kinds (`production`, `secrets`, `dns`, `identity`),
  two-level delegation (standing sphere handoff vs. per-packet execution),
  longest whole-segment prefix selection for overlapping subject matches on a
  packet `scope` with ancestor `advise`/`assure`/`interested` retention, the
  usable `project-work/v0` `scope` grammar subset (`<= 63` characters per
  segment, `<= 253` total, no `.` or `_`) vs. explicit profile mapping tables,
  and per-entry `exceptions[].right` uniqueness.

### `project-work/v0` commitment fields and `agent` / `run` party kinds

- **Packet commitment fields.** Extends `schemas/project-work/v0/ready-packet.schema.json`
  with optional `requested_by` (`person`, `role` or `agent` counterparty; never
  `unassigned` or `run`) and `check_by` (strict RFC 3339 `instant` with a
  required `T` time part and `Z` or UTC offset; required on non-terminal
  `run`-owned packets).
- **`agent` and `run` provenance.** Extends party records across
  `ready-packet.schema.json`, `project-state.schema.json`,
  `progress-event.schema.json` and `control-record.schema.json` with `agent`
  (`{kind, id, role, display?}`; `id` matches `^agent-[a-z0-9]+(-[a-z0-9]+)*$`,
  `role` required as the hat worn for the work) and `run`
  (`{kind, id, parent, expires, role?, display?}`; canonical uppercase ULID
  `id` with `run-` prefix and leading `0`–`7`, `parent` constrained to
  `agent-id` or `person-id` so runs cannot chain, and strict `instant`
  `expires`; allowed on packet `owner` and ledger `actor`, forbidden on
  `requested_by`, `project-state.owner` and `control-record.decider`).
- **Standardized `person-id` and semantic validation.** Defines a single
  `person-id` rule (`^[a-z][a-z0-9._-]{0,63}$`, excluding reserved `agent-` and
  `run-` prefixes and leading `@` handle punctuation) across all party and
  parent positions and adds
  [`schemas/project-work/v0/semantic-validation.md`](../../schemas/project-work/v0/semantic-validation.md)
  (`SEM-P01` through `SEM-P07` and `SEM-CASE-01` through `SEM-CASE-05`).

### Strategic `advisor` role (`1.0.0`, draft)

- Adds supplemental [`config/agentic/roles/advisor.yaml`](../../config/agentic/roles/advisor.yaml)
  (`1.0.0`, `status: draft`, category `consulting`) and
  [`docs/catalog/roles/advisor.md`](../catalog/roles/advisor.md) for client and
  stakeholder engagement guidance, problem framing and decision facilitation,
  with explicit boundary distinctions from `analyst`, `strategist`, `entarch`,
  `cxotech` and `prodmktg`.

### SPDX license split, pre-sign tagger identity enforcement and `durable-record` oracle fix

- **SPDX `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CC0` and `NOTICE`.** Replaces the combined Markdown
  explainer in `LICENSE` with verbatim SPDX `MIT` text (`LICENSE`) and verbatim
  SPDX `CC0-1.0` Universal legal code (`LICENSE-CC0`), moves the dual-license
  scope boundary (`CC0-1.0` for documentation, schemas, role prompts and
  conformance vectors/examples; `MIT` for executable scripts, CI workflows,
  `Makefile` and test harnesses), scope limitation and trademark notice to
  `NOTICE`, and sets `package.json` `"license"` to `"(MIT AND CC0-1.0)"`.
- **Pre-sign tagger identity and signing-subkey enforcement.** Adds
  `require_signing_identity` in `scripts/release-env.sh` and invokes it in
  `scripts/release-tag.sh` before any `gpg` call so
  `THREELEAPS_CRUCIBLE_TAGGER_NAME`, `THREELEAPS_CRUCIBLE_TAGGER_EMAIL` and
  `THREELEAPS_CRUCIBLE_PGP_KEY_ID` (`^[0-9A-F]{40}!$`) are required rather than
  warned-and-defaulted and verified against `config/release/tagger-identity.txt`
  and `docs/security/release-signing-keys.asc` before signing; runs
  `scripts/verify-pinned-tag.sh` immediately after tag creation; and includes
  `.immutable` verification in `RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`.
- **Durable-record wire-preflight ordering.** Updates
  `scripts/validate-durable-record-fixtures.py` to catch `OverflowError`
  alongside `ValueError` when materializing large non-protocol integer tokens
  above the IEEE 754 `binary64` finite range and completes the document-wide
  Stage 1 (`ingest`) pass before Stage 2 (`numeric_domain`) protocol-integer
  checks regardless of object member order.

## What a release carries

A crucible release is a GPG-signed annotated Git tag. It publishes no assets,
so there are no `SHA256SUMS` or `SHA512SUMS` files to verify. See
[`docs/security/README.md`](../security/README.md).

## Upgrade notes

- **For `project-work/v0` adopters (unstable `v0/`):** `requested_by`,
  `check_by` and the `agent`/`run` party kinds are additive, whereas tightening
  `person-id` from an arbitrary `1..128` string to `^[a-z][a-z0-9._-]{0,63}$`
  (excluding `agent-` and `run-`) rejects previously accepted values such as
  `@maintainer-1`, `human@example.test`, `Human1`, `agent-human` or 65-character
  ids. Adopters should keep historical ledger segments pinned to their
  write-time schema revision and remap legacy person ids via an explicit
  collision-checked identity-source mapping rather than blind in-place
  lowercasing or punctuation stripping.
- **For release tag operators:** `make release-tag` now requires
  `THREELEAPS_CRUCIBLE_TAGGER_NAME`, `THREELEAPS_CRUCIBLE_TAGGER_EMAIL` and
  `THREELEAPS_CRUCIBLE_PGP_KEY_ID` (`<40-hex>!`) to be exported in the
  environment and verified against `config/release/tagger-identity.txt` and
  `docs/security/release-signing-keys.asc` before invoking `gpg`.
